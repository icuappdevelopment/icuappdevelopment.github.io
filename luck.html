An Incremental Game About Engineering Fortune

Four luck machines, deep upgrade trees, and a prestige system that turns every collapse into a fresh burst of power.

Four Eras of Luck

  • Coin: Master the flip - streaks, crits, and overflips.
  • Dice: Chase combos and streak multipliers.
  • Deck: Deal piles of cards and stack their effects.
  • Spinner: Spin a wheel you slowly rig in your favor.

Blueprint Upgrade Trees

Every era has its own blueprint - a sprawling node tree where each purchase visibly changes how your luck machine behaves. Unlock new mechanics, boost payouts, and watch the numbers explode.

Laws, Collapse & Prestige

When your luck outgrows reality, collapse it in a full-screen cinematic and enact permanent Laws - powerful rules that persist across runs and let you push further with every reset.

Plays Offline, No Forced Ads

The game runs fully offline and your progress is saved on your device. Ads are strictly optional rewarded bonuses - you only ever watch one if you choose to. A welcome-back bonus greets you when you return.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Luck Architect?

Luck Architect is a free incremental game for Android where you engineer luck itself - flipping coins, rolling dice, drawing cards, and spinning wheels while upgrading blueprint trees and prestiging through reality collapses.

Is Luck Architect free?

Yes. The game is free to play. There are optional one-time purchases and optional rewarded ads, but no forced or interstitial ads.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The game plays fully offline. An internet connection is only needed if you choose to watch a rewarded ad or make a purchase.

Is it gambling?

No. Luck Architect is a game about probability mechanics - there is no real-money wagering and nothing to win or lose outside the game.
