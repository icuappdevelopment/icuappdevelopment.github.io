Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of workouts can I time?

Anything interval-based: HIIT, Tabata, boxing and MMA rounds, circuit training, EMOM-style sessions, stretching routines - if it has work and rest periods, you can build it.

Is the app free?

Yes. The free version includes the full timer and lets you save up to 3 workouts. A one-time premium purchase unlocks unlimited saved workouts and extra features - no subscription.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The timer works completely offline. Internet is only needed for purchases and restores.

Why does the app ask for camera access?

Only to scan workout QR codes shared by other users. Camera data is processed on-device and never uploaded.
