Luck Architect

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Introduction

Luck Architect is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app, how it is used, and your rights.

Information We Collect

  • We do not collect personal data, run our own analytics, or require an account.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Luck Architect shows optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. Ads only play when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus - there are no forced or interstitial ads. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address (which can indicate coarse location), and ad interaction data. This processing is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before any ad-related data is processed. You can review or change your choices at any time via Settings → Ad Privacy Options inside the app.

In-App Purchases

Optional one-time purchases and restores are handled entirely by the platform store provider (Google Play / Apple App Store). We never receive or store your payment details. Transaction-related data may be processed by the store provider under their own privacy policies.

Third-Party Services

Luck Architect uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded advertising and official platform billing services for purchases. It does not use any other third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Permissions

The app requires internet access for ads and purchases. It does not request sensitive permissions such as precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Children

Luck Architect is not directed at children. The app contains optional advertising and real-money in-app purchases and is intended for a general (non-child) audience.

Data Deletion & Your Rights

Because your save data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. For data processed by Google in connection with advertising, you can manage your preferences through the in-app consent settings and your device's ad settings, or exercise your rights directly with Google.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
